# Session 22B - GUI Control Audit

## Scope

This audit covers the local visual computer-use layer for Carter. Voice, webcam, camera streams, hidden operation, credential scraping, and security bypasses remain out of scope.

| Area | Status | Evidence | Decision |

## Breaches Closed

| Gap | Final State | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local structured visual observation missing | Closed | `src/carter_v2/universal/computer_use.py`: `VisualObservation`, `VisualElement`, `StructuredVisualObserver` |

| Missing S22B validation probes | Closed | `probe_all_tools.py` adds `S22B-GUI-1` through `S22B-GUI-6` |
